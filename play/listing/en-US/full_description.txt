Kindred is a dating app made for Sierra Leone. Whether you are in Freetown, Bo, Kenema, Makeni or Koidu, Kindred helps you meet genuine people who share your values — for a relationship, marriage or new friendships.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create your profile in about a minute: your photos, a short bio, your interests and the languages you speak — Krio, English, Temne, Mende, Limba, Fula and more.
• Swipe right to like, left to pass, or send a Super Like when someone really stands out.
• When you both like each other, it's a match — and you can start chatting straight away.
• Send photos and short videos, or make a free voice or video call right from the chat.
• Filter by age and by town, or see people from all over Salone.

MADE FOR SALONE
• Built for local life: towns across all regions, local languages and what people here are actually looking for, from friendship to marriage.
• Light on data: photos are compressed on your phone before upload, and the app remembers what it has already loaded, so it keeps working on slow connections.
• Choose whether to show your religion — it's always optional.

SAFETY FIRST
• Adults only: every member must be 18 or older.
• Your exact date of birth is never shown — only your age.
• Block, unmatch or report anyone in two taps. They are not told who reported them.
• Built-in safety tips, including a clear rule: never send money, Orange Money, Afrimoney or airtime to someone you have not met.
• You can only message or call people who have matched with you.
• Photos and videos you receive stay blurred until you tap to see them.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• We never sell your data.
• Edit your profile at any time.
• Delete your account and everything in it — photos, matches and messages — directly from the app.

Kindred is free to use. It works on your phone and on the web at kindred-sl.netlify.app, so you can chat from either.
