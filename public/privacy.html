Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 (chat photos, videos and calls added)

Kindred is a dating app for adults in Sierra Leone, available on Android and at kindred-sl.netlify.app. It is published by Kindred ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts or your phone's call log. To tell your devices apart, the app creates its own random ID on each device. It is not your phone's hardware ID and is never used for advertising. Kindred has no ads, and we do not sell your data.

2. Who at Kindred can see your data

A small moderation team can see member accounts (including email addresses) in order to handle reports, remove rule-breaking photos and suspend accounts. To protect members from scams, messages are also checked automatically for signs of fraud, such as requests for money, Orange Money, Afrimoney or airtime, or attempts to move the chat to WhatsApp or a phone number. Only messages caught by these checks, and messages attached to a report, are shown to moderators. Moderators can open a chat photo or video only when it is part of a report. They cannot listen to or watch calls. Every moderation action is recorded in an internal log.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase (database, login, and photo and video storage) on servers in London, United Kingdom. The website is hosted by Netlify. These companies process data only on our behalf, to run the service. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Profile photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses so that they load quickly on slow connections. Anyone who is given the exact address of a photo could open it, so only upload photos you are comfortable showing on a public dating profile.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages and call history straight away, and the photos and videos from those chats shortly after. The same happens to a chat's photos and videos when either of you unmatches. Reports you sent about other members are kept so that moderators can act on them, but your name is removed from them.

5. Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile at any time in Profile → Edit profile.
  • Block, unmatch or report anyone from their profile or chat.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the steps at kindred-sl.netlify.app/delete-account.html.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask any question about privacy, at kindred.salone@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

6. Adults only

Kindred is only for people aged 18 or over. We check date of birth when you sign up and do not knowingly allow anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 is using Kindred, report them in the app or email us, and we will remove the account.

7. Security

Every account is protected by row-level security in our database, so members can only read the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
