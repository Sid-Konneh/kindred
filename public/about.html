About Kindred

Helping Salone find its kindred spirits

Kindred is a dating app made for Sierra Leone. It's for adults who want to meet genuine people who share their values, whether that leads to friendship, a relationship or marriage.

kindred/ˈkɪndrɪd/ · adjective

Similar in kind; related. A kindred spirit is someone who understands you: someone who shares your values, your sense of humour and the things that matter most to you. Kindred is built to help you find that person.

Why Kindred

Dating that fits life in Salone

Most dating apps were designed for other countries. We built Kindred around the people, places and phones of Sierra Leone.

🗺️

Local from the start

Towns in every region, the languages people actually speak, and room to say what you're looking for, from new friends to marriage.

📶

Made for real connections

The app is light on data. Photos are compressed on your phone, and what you've already seen stays saved, so Kindred keeps working on a slow 3G signal.

🛡️

Safety you can see

Money scams are a real risk on dating apps. Kindred warns about them inside every chat and makes blocking and reporting two taps away.

Our commitments

How we keep Kindred safe

These rules are built into the app itself, not just written in a policy.

  • 18
    Adults onlyEvery member confirms their date of birth, and the app refuses anyone under 18. The date can't be changed later.
  • 💬
    Messages only between matchesNobody can message you unless you have both said yes.
  • 🚫
    Block and report in two tapsBlocked people disappear from your matches and can no longer find you. Reports go to the Kindred team for review, and the person is never told who reported them.
  • 🗑️
    Real deletionDeleting your account removes your profile, photos, matches and messages straight away. How to delete.
