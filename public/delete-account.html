Delete your Kindred account

You can delete your Kindred account and all of its data at any time. You do not need the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app or on the website (instant)

  1. Open the Kindred app, or go to kindred-sl.netlify.app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you cannot sign in, email kindred.salone@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Kindred account". We will delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Deleted permanently and straight away: your login, profile, all photos, likes and passes, matches, all messages you sent and received, and your blocks.

Kept, with your name removed: reports you submitted about other members, so moderators can still act on them.
